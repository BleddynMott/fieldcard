Round 2 · three ways to make the call

Same Sports day look, same five events, same card #41. Only the call changes. In all three, it's how well you know your own game against everyone else's, so you get better at it over the days. No other daily found so far does this.

OPTION 1 · RECOMMENDED

Set the bar

Before each event, set your own bar like a high jumper: beat half the field (1 pt), three in four (2 pts) or nine in ten (4 pts). Play. Clear the bar and you score it; knock it and you score nothing. Most points out of 20.

  • Why: every event has a decision, stakes and a reveal, five times a run. The bar is the theme and the scoring at once. Going for gold on your best event is the story people tell.
  • Draws on: Field par (a call before each hole) and The deal (stakes you choose).
  • Risk: one choice of three before each game adds about 3 seconds an event.

OPTION 2

Over or under par

Before each event: Beat the field or Not today. Play. Par is the field's middle score. Called right scores 1. Out of 5.

  • Why: the simplest call possible: one tap, readable by anyone. The golf "par" idea you liked in C, kept in words.
  • Draws on: Field par, as shortlisted.
  • Risk: a yes/no call has no risk to weigh, so strong players just say "beat it" every time. It can go flat by the second week.

OPTION 3

Pin your ribbons

Play all five. Before any results go up, pin a gold (3), silver (2) and bronze (1) ribbon on three events. A ribbon pays if you beat par there. Out of 6.

  • Why: keeps one step at the end, like the ordering, but with stakes, and only three things to place, not five.
  • Draws on: The deal, as shortlisted.
  • Risk: it is still a step after the fun part, the kind of final stage you found unclear in the Lovable version.

Looked at and not shown
