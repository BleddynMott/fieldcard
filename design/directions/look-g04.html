A · Finals day

Sports day, evolved. Same paper, ink, lawn and type you picked.

  • The stake is a rosette with the number on it.
  • The crowd are heads on the bank.
  • Bold is a bigger rosette.

B · For keeps

Break time. Chalk on tarmac, playing marbles for keeps.

  • The stake is your yellow marbles: you can count them.
  • The crowd are blue marbles, one each.
  • Bold is more marbles in the ring.

C · The Ridge

A climb, printed flat in two inks.

  • The stake is how high your flag stands.
  • The crowd is the mountain: a pile is a hill.
  • Bold is higher.
