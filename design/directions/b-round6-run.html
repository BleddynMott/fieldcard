Rules behind the draft

The Hot Seat. Each day one player from yesterday who chose to be eligible, shown only by town, time and streak. One tap: did they make their claim? It counts toward Hunch. The seated player sees tonight how everyone bet on them.

Spot it (Three Doors). One tap on one event, which rotates daily. Right if you pick the door most of the crowd went through.

Draw it (Pour the Sand). Five boxes; one bucket of sand to share between them by holding your finger over a box. Score: the share of your sand that sits where the crowd's sand also sits, 0 to 100.

Claim it (Join the Queue). 20 figures, each 5 in 100 of everyone. Step in where you think you finished. Get there or further: score your claim. Fall short: lose 2 for every point short.

Hands is your place in the five events, out of 100. Hunch is your place among everyone at the Hot Seat, Spot it and Draw it, out of 100. Claim points are kept apart and feed the Sunday character.

Mirror Duel. Each member is paired with a random member of today's rival team; higher Hunch wins the duel. The team that wins the larger share of duels pulls the rope. Using a share, not a count, means a small team is not beaten by size alone.

Class Photo. Every Sunday: up to 60 figures drawn from the week's field, rows by weekly Hunch, front row best. Your figure is the coloured one.
