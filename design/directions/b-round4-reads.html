Draw the crowd

Like Extraplot: drag your finger across the chart to draw how the crowd's guesses spread. Reveal in three layers: the real crowd as bars, everyone else's drawings as a faint line, and yours dashed. Score: how much of your shape overlaps the real one.

One stroke
Score
Overlap %, and where you sit among the readers
For
The richest reveal; you see the crowd and the crowd's idea of itself
Against

Tug of war

Two teams pull: the undercounters and the overcounters. Drag the knot to where you think the rope settled. Then the rope moves to the real split, and a ghost knot shows where readers on average put it. It's sports day in one gesture.

Input
One drag
Score
How close your knot was
For
Readable from across a room; works on every event (early v late on Stop, too short v too long on Repeat)
Against
Only one split a day; the "spot on" people sit out

Spot the real crowd

Four charts of how the crowd guessed. Three are fakes, each wrong in one believable way. Tap the real one. Then see how many readers picked each.

For
The fastest; very easy to start; the fakes can teach (too centred, too spread, the wrong way)

Thinner skill: a 1-in-4 guess hits a quarter of the time

How they could combine

1, 2, 4 and 5 can feed the team flag: each read averages across a team, so the team whose average is closest wins the fixture. 3 is about you, so it fits better as the last beat before the Scorecard than as the team read. 6 could be a quick warm-up in place of the trap question.
