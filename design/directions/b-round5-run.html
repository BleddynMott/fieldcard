The rules behind it

Claim it. You claim a place c (you beat c% of the field). Your real place is p. If p ≥ c you score +c. If p < c you score −2 × (c − p). So a claim of 90 that lands pays 90, and the same claim at 80 costs 20. The best claim is a little under where you really expect to be, and it pays more the better you know yourself.

Personal record: claim points add up over the week; the best week is your record. Team: members' claim points add to a weekly "nerve" table alongside the reading fixtures.

The character

Over at least four days in a week. Nerve: your average claim minus your average place. More than 8 above is Show-off, more than 8 below is Dark Horse, otherwise Straight. Reading: is your drawing nearer to the other readers' average or to the real crowd? Nearer the readers: Follower. Away from the readers and nearer the crowd: Scout. Otherwise Mixed. That gives 3 × 3 = 9 characters.

Length

The events take about 2 minutes; Spot it, Draw it and Claim it about 30 seconds together; the Scorecard 15. About 3 minutes in all. Spot it is the one to cut if a run feels long.
