Five quick tests. After each one, guess how everyone else did on it. Plant your flag, then the results come in.

Sports day. Every screen has one job. Other players are "everyone" or "the crowd". Three scores: Hands (the events), Hunch (reading the crowd: the five reads and the Hot Seat) and your Flag.

Hot Seat→Stop + read→Count + read→Repeat + read→Pair + read→Sort + read→Claim it→the results→Scorecard→Your week so far…Sunday…Tomorrow's home
About 4 minutes: tests 2 min, reads 40 s, results 1 min

The four reads are the Read of the day forms, one after each event: Find yourself (Stop), Draw the crowd (Count, the drawn form of Pour the sand), Pick the crowd (Repeat), Fix the crowd (Sort). Pair gets the read he said was fine "as just something asked after the round" (round 3).

New names, open to change: Find yourself, Pick the crowd, Fix the crowd, Draw the crowd; The Fold (this option); "tests" (the rules line's word; the screens say events); The trap (Pair's read: which shape fooled the most people); the results; Your week so far; nerve (the team claim table, from round 5); Tomorrow's home.

Comes from: Field par's call on every hole (M03), the four distribution reads he asked for (25 Nov, round 6), Draw the crowd (round 4, option 2), Where did they go wrong (R1), Claim it, the Hot Seat, Mirror Duels, the Class Photo.
