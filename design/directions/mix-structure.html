EVENTREADBoth: the read happens inside an event.
Untagged: the Scorecard (7) and the Class Photo (9-2). They show results, not a test or a guess.

The card has one shape every day (slots 1 to 9 below). Two slots change by day: 4, Today's twist (the crowd inside one event) and 5, Read of the day (one read after the five). Shared screens are shown once; where a slot has options, each option is a column you can keep, cut or change.

One made-up day across the whole page: card #41, Wed 25 Nov, you counted 15 on Count (the truth is 17), stopped 1.2% off on Stop, Hands 57, Hunch 82, flag 70. Every option is drawn on this same day's numbers so they compare like with like; the week table (0a) says which day each would really run. Numbers beyond those are illustrative, not modelled. Crowd numbers say "so far" and settle at midnight.

New names, open to change: Today's twist; The Herd; The Heat (watch first); Read the hand; Crowd ghost; Draw the crowd; Find yourself; Pick the crowd; Fix the crowd; The trap (which shape fooled most); Tomorrow's home; "how it went" (Claim it's five results); the EVENT and READ tags.
