R1 · RECOMMENDED

Where did they go wrong?

After each event, the board you just played comes back. One tap: where the crowd went wrong. Then the board lights up with everyone's taps. Crowd's top spot scores 2, runner-up 1. Out of 10.

  • Per event: Stop, where most people stopped · Count, the crowd's favourite wrong number · Repeat, the square where most people broke · Pair, the shape that fooled most people · Sort, the two blobs most people swapped.
  • Why "wow": every reveal is a small, true fact about how people see ("41% fell for this one, including you"). That's what people screenshot and pass on.
  • Why anyone can top it: knowing people beats fast hands. A slow player who understands the crowd's habits tops the Readers board. People's slips repeat from day to day, so readers improve, and that improvement is the reason to come back.
  • Came from: four of the six workers reached it independently.

R2

Draw the crowd

After each event, drag a hill along the track: where you think everyone landed. Then the real crowd runs in as little figures and stacks up where they truly finished. You score the overlap.

  • Why: your bell-curve hunch, made into something you can touch. The crowd literally runs onto the track.
  • Against: drawing a spread with a thumb is the least familiar thing in the set, and five draws make the run long. I'd use it for one event a day, not all five.
