## What the references changed

Perceptle is the nearest existing game found so far: five tiny tests, one per screen, then each shown as "beats N% of players". The scan of 24 Nov did not find it. What Fieldcard has that it does not is the call: the player orders their five games by where they think they beat the field *before* any percentile is shown. The niche rests on that step and on a theme that carries it.
