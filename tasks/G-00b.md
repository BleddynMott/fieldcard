## Short spec

The spec as first written (before any mock-up) is in git history at the task's first commit. This is the spec as the options now stand, after review rounds 1 and 2.

**In all three** (richest approved version per R5):
- The five events, Perceptle-like, played at the truth (Hands): Stop, Count, Repeat, Pair, Sort ("mixing graph one with perceptle", 26 Nov).
- Claim it, the flag with the bead chart, plus round 5's week claim record and team nerve (`b-round5-run.html`; his "the flag was alright", 17:43, refers back to round 5).

- Teams and the rope (Mirror Duel). Results settle at midnight, and Tomorrow's home shows what settled.
- Crowd psychology as what everyone thought against what happened, from the day's numbers. Plus at most one general line per reveal (R2, his to sign).
- New names are listed in each intro for his approval. The Sports day look and `tokens.css` colours.

**A. The Fold.** The Hot Seat as in round 6, with its reveal. Every event is two answers: your answer, then your read of everyone on the same event. Count = Draw the crowd (Extraplot, three layers plus the truth), Stop = Find yourself, Repeat = Pick the crowd, Sort = Fix the crowd, Pair = which shape fooled the most people (R1, which he said was fine "as just something asked after the round", 15:52). Then you plant your flag, and the results come in, in play order. Open question for him: five reads on every card, against his "day to day" (17:43) and "not repeated 5 times" (15:52).

**B. The Heat.** You run every event in a heat against four players who opted in and finished early today. Labelled pacers run until those four finish. Before each event you back one of the four (not yourself). During the event the ghosts run beside you. After it: the heat result with everyone's backing drawn per lane, and "the four will see tonight how everyone backed them". After the five events comes a Read of the day with the four forms day to day (today Draw the crowd, Extraplot layers, with the four and you as pins), then Claim it, then who everyone backed. The heat takes the Hot Seat's place. Comes from L2 Heat of Five, E06 and O11 (never shown) and C8 The Hot Seat.

**C. The Experiment.** The Hot Seat on home, as in round 6. Each card runs one social experiment on everyone, with the same twist for all (P08). Monday, The Herd: after Count you see the first five opted-in answers of the day and may change yours. You guess how many in 100 moved toward the five. The reveal sets first answers against final answers, the truth, the five, you, and everyone's guesses. On the other events, one tap on the event's own board for where the crowd went wrong (R1's concrete forms). The flag is the standing experiment in overconfidence. Sunday reads the week's experiments back as your character, on C's own reading axis. Comes from S3, S7, C6, W6, QE01 (never shown) and R1.
